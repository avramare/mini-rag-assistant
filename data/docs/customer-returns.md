---
id: customer-returns
title: Customer returns process
access: public
---
Customers can return parcels within 30 days of delivery. Returns are collected on the next scheduled route in the customer's area, not by a dedicated trip. A returned parcel must be scanned at the depot within 24 hours of pickup. Damaged returns are photographed and stored in the claims folder for 2 years. Customers receive a refund confirmation by email once the return is scanned.
