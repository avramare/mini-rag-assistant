title: Expense policy
access: public
---
Business expenses must be submitted in the Ledgerly tool within 30 days of the purchase. Receipts are required for any expense above 25 euros. The daily meal allowance on business trips is 45 euros. Train travel is preferred for trips under 500 kilometres; flights for such trips need a manager's written approval. Taxi rides are reimbursed only between 22:00 and 06:00 or when carrying equipment. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
