access: public
---
New employees receive their laptop and access badge on their first day from the IT service desk. In the first week every new hire completes the safety induction, which takes 3 hours. Each new employee is assigned a buddy for the first 3 months. The probation period is 2 months for all contracts. HR schedules a check-in conversation at the end of the first month.
