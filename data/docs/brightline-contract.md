access: restricted

Kestrel's largest customer, Brightline Retail, pays 3.85 euros per standard parcel under the current contract. The contract runs until 30 June 2028 and includes a volume discount of 6 percent above 2 million parcels per year. A penalty of 15,000 euros applies for each week that on-time delivery falls below 95 percent. The contract was signed by the Chief Commercial Officer.
