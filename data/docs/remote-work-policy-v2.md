title: Remote work policy
access: public
effective: 2026-06-01
---
Office staff at Kestrel Logistics may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Warehouse and fleet roles are not eligible for remote work. Remote days must be registered in the Roster app by Friday noon for the following week. The company pays a home-office allowance of 30 euros per month to eligible staff. Remote work from outside the country is allowed for at most 15 working days per year and requires HR approval in advance.
