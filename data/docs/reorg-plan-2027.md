title: Reorganisation plan 2027
access: restricted
---
The management team plans to merge the Venlo and Tilburg dispatch teams into a single team in Tilburg by March 2027. The plan affects 18 dispatcher positions, of which 6 are expected to become redundant. The works council will be informed in November 2026. The internal project name for the reorganisation is FALCONRIDGE. Employees must not be told before the works council has been consulted.
