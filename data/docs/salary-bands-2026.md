access: restricted
---
Salary band D1 for delivery drivers ranges from 2,650 to 3,100 euros gross per month. Band W2 for warehouse shift leads ranges from 3,300 to 3,900 euros. Band M3 for department heads ranges from 6,200 to 7,800 euros. The general salary increase for 2026 is 3.4 percent, effective 1 April. Bonuses are capped at 8 percent of annual salary.
