On 11 February 2026 a phishing email led to the compromise of one dispatcher account. The attacker accessed the Roster app for 47 minutes before the account was locked. No customer data was exported. The incident was reported to the data protection authority on 13 February under reference SI-2026-017. As a follow-up, SMS-based login codes were replaced by the Keyloop authenticator app.
