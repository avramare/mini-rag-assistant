id: holiday-policy
title: Holiday policy
access: public
effective: 2025-01-01
---
Kestrel Logistics employees receive 27 days of paid holiday per calendar year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, but they expire on 31 March. Holiday requests longer than 10 consecutive working days need approval from a department head. New employees accrue holiday pro rata from their start date. During the peak season from 15 November to 31 December, warehouse staff may take at most 3 days of holiday.
