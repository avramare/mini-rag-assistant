title: Sustainability report 2025
access: public
---
In 2025 Kestrel Logistics emitted 18,400 tonnes of CO2, a reduction of 9 percent compared with 2024. The target is net-zero last-mile delivery by 2032. Solar panels on the Tilburg warehouse roof produced 1.1 gigawatt-hours of electricity in 2025. Packaging waste is sorted into 5 streams at every depot. The report was reviewed by an external auditor, GreenLedger Audits.
