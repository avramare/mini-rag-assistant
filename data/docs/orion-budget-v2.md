On 10 September 2026 the board approved a revised budget for Project Orion of 5 million euros over two years, effective 1 January 2027. Until then the current budget remains in force. Under the revised budget, 2 million euros is allocated to the external development partner, Vantor Systems. The internal codename for the pilot phase is BLUEHERON. If the pilot misses its 15 percent target, the budget for phase two will be cut by 45 percent.
