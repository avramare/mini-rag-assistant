title: Meeting room booking
access: public
---
Meeting rooms in the Tilburg office are booked through the Roster app. Bookings are limited to 2 hours unless approved by facilities. A room that is not checked in within 10 minutes of the start time is released automatically. The largest room, called Harbour, seats 24 people and has video conferencing. Catering must be ordered at least 1 working day in advance.
