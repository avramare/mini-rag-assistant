access: public

Kestrel Logistics operates 212 delivery vans and 38 heavy trucks. Since 2025, 64 of the vans are fully electric. Every vehicle has a telematics box that reports position every 30 seconds. Vans are serviced every 25,000 kilometres or every 12 months, whichever comes first. The main fleet depot is in Tilburg, and a second smaller depot opened in Venlo in 2026.
