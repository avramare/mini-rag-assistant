id: it-password-policy
title: Password and login policy
access: public
---
All Kestrel accounts require a password of at least 14 characters. Passwords do not expire on a schedule, but must be changed immediately after a suspected compromise. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the Roster app and the VPN; the approved method is the Keyloop authenticator app, not SMS. After 8 failed login attempts an account is locked for 30 minutes. The IT service desk will never ask for your password.
