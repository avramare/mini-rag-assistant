access: restricted
---
The approved budget for Project Orion is 4.2 million euros over two years. 1.6 million euros is allocated to the external development partner, Vantor Systems. The internal codename for the pilot phase is BLUEHERON. If the pilot misses its 12 percent target, the budget for phase two will be cut by 40 percent. The budget was approved by the board on 14 March 2026.
