Project Orion is building a new route planning system for Kestrel's delivery vans. The goal is to reduce average kilometres per delivery by 12 percent. The pilot runs in the Venlo region with 20 vans. Orion replaces the old planner called RouteMax, which will be switched off after the pilot succeeds. The project sponsor is the Chief Operations Officer. Questions about Orion go to the team channel #orion-dev.
