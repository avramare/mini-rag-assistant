---
id: driver-safety-rules
title: Driver safety rules
access: public
---
Drivers may drive at most 9 hours per day and must take a 45-minute break after 4.5 hours of driving. Mobile phones may only be used hands-free. Any collision, however minor, must be reported in the Roster app within 2 hours. Drivers complete a refresher safety course every 24 months. Loads above 20 kilograms must be moved with a trolley or lift, never carried alone.
