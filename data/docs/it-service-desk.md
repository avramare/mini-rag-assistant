access: public
---
The IT service desk is reachable by ticket in the Helpwise portal or by phone on extension 4400. Opening hours are 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays. Priority 1 incidents, such as a full outage of the dispatch system, have a response target of 15 minutes. Priority 3 requests, such as a new monitor, have a response target of 3 working days. Laptops are replaced every 4 years.
