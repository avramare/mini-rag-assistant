title: Warehouse shifts
access: public
---
The Tilburg warehouse runs three shifts: early from 06:00 to 14:00, late from 14:00 to 22:00 and night from 22:00 to 06:00. Night shifts are paid with a 30 percent supplement. Shift swaps must be agreed between both employees and logged in the Roster app at least 48 hours in advance. Each shift has one shift lead who is the first contact for safety issues. The warehouse is closed on 25 December and 1 January.
